Prepare the Google Play release checklist for the ScoreLab Android app using the `play-store-release` skill.

Done when:

- every item in the skill's build checks, policy and testing sections is marked done, to-do (with owner) or needs the founder;
- the current Google Play target-SDK requirement and closed-testing rules for new accounts are checked and written down;
- anything you need from the founder (app source access, signing key, Play Console access) is listed as exact steps.

Move to review for the founder when done.
