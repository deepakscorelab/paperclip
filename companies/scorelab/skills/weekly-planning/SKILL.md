# Weekly planning

## 1. Review last week

- What was finished and approved?
- What is still in review waiting for the founder? List these first so the founder can clear them.
- What is blocked, and who can unblock it?
- Spending so far this month for each agent.

## 2. Pick at most 5 priorities

Rank by impact on the company goals: Play Store launch, question-bank size and quality, daily current affairs, and social channel growth. Exam calendars matter: move RAS/RPSC, UPSC or SSC work up when an exam or notification is near.

## 3. Delegate

For each priority, create a child task with:

- a clear title,
- the owner (Content Lead, Social Media Manager or Engineer),
- what "done" means (for example "60 RAS Rajasthan-polity questions, verified, as CSV, in review"),
- any deadline.

## 4. Post the plan

Write one comment on the planning task:

- **Waiting for you (founder):** items to approve or decide
- **This week:** the priorities, with owners
- **Blocked:** item → who unblocks → what they need to do
- **Budget:** anything close to its limit
