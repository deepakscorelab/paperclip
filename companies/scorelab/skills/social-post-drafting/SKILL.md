# Social post drafting

Every post is a **draft for the founder's approval**. Never publish or schedule anything.

## Channel formats

| Channel | What to draft |
| --- | --- |
| Telegram | Quiz polls (question + 4 options + correct answer + one-line explanation), short current-affairs notes, test announcements |
| Instagram | Carousels (slide-by-slide text, 5–8 slides), reel scripts (15–45 seconds), caption with a call to action and 8–15 relevant hashtags |
| YouTube | Shorts scripts (under 60 seconds) with title and description; longer video outlines with chapters |
| WhatsApp Channel | Short daily update: 3–5 lines plus a link to the ScoreLab test |

## Daily post pack (typical)

1. Telegram: 3–5 quiz polls from today's current-affairs digest.
2. Instagram: one carousel ("Top 5 current affairs for RAS today") or one reel script.
3. YouTube: one Shorts script.
4. WhatsApp Channel: one daily update.
5. One "take a test on ScoreLab" post with a link to scorelab.co.in or the app.

## Rules

- Use only facts from approved Content Lead material.
- Bilingual: Hindi first for RAS/RPSC audiences, English first for UPSC/SSC posts, or both side by side.
- Tone: friendly, motivating, honest. No promises of selection, no fake urgency, no copying other creators' content, no copyrighted music or images.
- Image and video ideas go in a short brief (what to show, text on screen, colours), not as finished designs.
- Suggest a posting time in India time for each post.

## Voiceovers

For reels and Shorts, include a voiceover script. If ElevenLabs is configured, generate the audio as a draft and attach it; otherwise leave the script for the founder.

## Deliver

One post-pack document per day, one section per post, each with: channel, language, text, media brief, hashtags and suggested time. Then move the task to review.
