# Current-affairs digest

## Pick the right news

Choose 8–12 items from the previous day that could plausibly be asked in an exam:

- **Rajasthan** (always include 2–4 items when available): state government schemes, appointments, budget, places, culture, awards, sports.
- **National:** government schemes and policies, constitutional and legal developments, economy (RBI, budget, indices), science and technology, defence, environment, awards and appointments.
- **International:** summits, agreements, rankings and reports that mention India.

Skip political opinion, gossip, crime stories and anything without exam value. Stay neutral: report what happened, not whether it is good or bad.

## Verify

Prefer primary sources: PIB, official ministry and state government sites (rajasthan.gov.in), RBI, and official reports. A news site is acceptable as a pointer, but confirm key facts (names, numbers, dates) from an official source where possible. Record the source link for every item.

## Format each item

- **Headline** (English and Hindi)
- **What happened** — 2–3 sentences
- **Why it matters for the exam** — the static topic it links to (for example "links to Fundamental Rights, Article 21")
- **Key facts to remember** — 2–4 bullet points (names, numbers, places)
- **Practice question** — 1–2 MCQs in the `exam-question-writing` format
- **Source** — link

## Deliver

A single digest document titled `Current Affairs — <date>`, plus the practice questions as a CSV attachment. Then move the task to review for the founder's approval.
